Design Concepts

Both concepts use the same website structure and functionality while exploring two different approaches to visual presentation. Each one presents the identical wireframe — the same pages, navigation, resource content, search, and filters — styled in a distinct visual direction.

COA 1

Vanderbilt Concept

A Vanderbilt-style presentation without Vanderbilt branding. Institutional and editorial, with serif display headings, a strong black header, and gold accents.

View Vanderbilt Concept
COA 2

Warm & Child-Centered

A distinct visual identity using a warmer, more approachable design system. Teal, coral, and golden-yellow tones with rounded cards and friendly typography.

View Warm Concept

What stays the same

These concepts are meant to be compared directly. The page sections, navigation labels, content ordering, filters, resource cards, search behavior, and resource detail layout are identical across both. The differences are presentational: typography, color, spacing, card and button styling, borders, and overall tone.
