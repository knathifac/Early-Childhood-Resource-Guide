About this guide

This prototype explores a public directory of early childhood educational resources for parents and caregivers, educators and practitioners, and researchers and scholars.

Demonstration content only

All 15 listings are fictional examples for reviewing the guide’s organization and behavior. Sample materials are unavailable. No institutional affiliation, endorsement, research finding, or educational outcome is claimed.

Final content, age definitions, wording, and any affiliations are pending client review.

Using the guide

  1. Browse by topic or search titles, descriptions, and metadata.
  2. Select filters. Multiple choices in one group match any choice; different groups must all match.
  3. Open a listing to review its description, intended audience, and material availability.

Share or bookmark the directory URL to keep your search and filters.

Prototype scope

This wireframe has no accounts, tracking, submissions, or backend. The interface is in English; Spanish and bilingual labels describe sample resource metadata.
